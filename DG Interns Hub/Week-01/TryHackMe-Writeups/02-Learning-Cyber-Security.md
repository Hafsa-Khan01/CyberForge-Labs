# TryHackMe Write-up — Learning Cyber Security

## Objective
Understand the main cybersecurity domains, the purpose of security controls and the responsibilities of security professionals.

## Concepts
- Confidentiality, integrity and availability form the CIA triad.
- Prevention reduces the chance of an incident; detection identifies suspicious activity; response limits damage and restores operations.
- Governance defines policies, ownership, risk appetite and accountability.
- Technical security depends on people, process and technology working together.

## Learning Reflection
Cybersecurity is broader than penetration testing. Defensive monitoring, secure configuration, identity management, incident response, awareness and risk management are all part of the discipline.
