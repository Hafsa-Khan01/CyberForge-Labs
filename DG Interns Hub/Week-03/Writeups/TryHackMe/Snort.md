# TryHackMe — Snort

**Room:** https://tryhackme.com/room/snort

## Objective
Understand Snort's role in network monitoring, packet inspection, rule evaluation and alert interpretation.

## Rule-analysis checklist
- Action and protocol.
- Source/destination addresses and ports.
- Direction operator.
- Message, SID and revision.
- Content, flags, flow and threshold conditions.
- Classification and references.
