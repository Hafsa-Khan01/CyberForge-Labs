## Learning focus
Understand how a SIEM centralizes security telemetry and supports search, correlation, alerting, investigation and reporting.

## SIEM lifecycle
1. Collect endpoint, network, identity and application events.
2. Parse and normalize records.
3. Enrich with asset, identity and threat-intelligence context.
4. Correlate events and evaluate detection rules.
5. Present searchable events and alerts.
6. Retain evidence and support reporting.
