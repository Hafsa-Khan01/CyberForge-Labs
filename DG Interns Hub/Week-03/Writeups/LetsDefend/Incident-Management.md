# LetsDefend — Incident Management

**Location:** LetsDefend training library

## Learning focus
Understand the lifecycle used to track, prioritize, coordinate and close security incidents.

## Typical lifecycle
Detection/reporting; triage and severity assignment; investigation and scoping; containment, eradication and recovery; communication/escalation; evidence preservation and post-incident review.
