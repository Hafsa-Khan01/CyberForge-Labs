## Learning focus
Investigate suspicious Linux host activity while preserving useful evidence.

## Evidence sources
- /var/log/auth.log or journal equivalents — authentication.
- /var/log/syslog and journalctl — system/service events.
- /var/log/audit/audit.log — auditd telemetry when configured.
- ps and ss — process and network state.
- Services and scheduled tasks — potential persistence locations.
