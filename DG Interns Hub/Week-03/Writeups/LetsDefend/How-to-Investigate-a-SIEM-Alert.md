# LetsDefend — How to Investigate a SIEM Alert

**Lesson:** https://app.letsdefend.io/training/lessons/how-to-investigate-a-siem-alert

## Objective
Validate an alert, gather supporting telemetry, assess scope and impact, and document a defensible disposition.

## Analyst workflow
1. Review detection logic, severity, timestamp, asset and triggering evidence.
2. Validate the alert against raw events.
3. Pivot on host, user, process, IP, domain and time.
4. Assess whether activity is expected, suspicious or confirmed malicious.
5. Determine scope and related alerts.
6. Record rationale, evidence and escalation/containment recommendation.
