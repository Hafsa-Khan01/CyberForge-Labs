# Week 05 Findings and Evidence

Store original screenshots, exported JSON and text outputs here. Use UTC or clearly record local timezone (PKT, UTC+05:00). Redact personal addresses, tokens and unrelated account information.

## Suggested layout
- Phishing-01/, Phishing-02/, Phishing-03/
- EICAR/
- Public-Sandbox-01/, Public-Sandbox-02/
- ATTACK-Navigator/
- Atomic-T1059.001/, Atomic-T1136.001/, Atomic-T1053.005/

## Evidence naming
`YYYYMMDD_HHMMPKT_<case>_<artifact>.<ext>`

## Rules
- Preserve original evidence; analyse copies.
- Record source, collection time, hash and analyst.
- Do not place live malware in this repository.
- Do not upload confidential email or attachments to public reputation services.
- The records below are templates until populated with genuine evidence.
