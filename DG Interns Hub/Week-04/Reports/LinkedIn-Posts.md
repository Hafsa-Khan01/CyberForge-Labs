## Post B — Detection engineering focus

A useful SIEM alert is more than a search query.

It needs a reliable log source, validated fields, a clear detection condition, an appropriate threshold, context for triage and evidence that the rule behaves as intended.

During my SIEM practice, I explored authentication monitoring, endpoint telemetry, SPL searches, Wazuh rule logic and the investigation process for suspicious activity.

My biggest takeaway: detection engineering is iterative. Validate the data first, test the logic, investigate benign explanations and tune carefully without losing visibility.
