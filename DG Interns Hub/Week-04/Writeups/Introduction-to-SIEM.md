# TryHackMe — Introduction to SIEM

**Room:** https://tryhackme.com/room/introtosiem  
**Completion status:** Completed

## Learning outcomes
- Understand the purpose and core components of a SIEM.
- Follow the flow from event collection and parsing to searchable data.
- Understand correlation, alert generation, enrichment and tuning.
- Relate SIEM alerts to the SOC investigation and triage lifecycle.

## Practical relevance
SIEM effectiveness depends on reliable telemetry, correct timestamps, useful field extraction and appropriate detection logic. Alerts are investigation leads and must be validated against supporting events and normal activity.
